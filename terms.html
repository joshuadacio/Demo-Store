Last updated: November 2026.

Refunds

All of our services come with a 30-day refund window. To ask for a refund, contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.

There is one exception. Consulting offers are not refundable. A consulting offer is one where what you receive is advice or a diagnosis, not something we build. We state this on the offer before you pay. Our consulting offers are:

  • Business Automation Audit
  • AI Readiness Assessment
  • Growth Support Plan
  • Technology Support Plan
  • Funnel Review

30 Days of Store Content is something we build for you, so it falls under the 30-day refund window.

Revisions

Each engagement includes up to two revision rounds. The second round is included, not an exception. A third round is quoted separately or moved to the next cycle.

Delivery

The delivery clock starts when your intake is complete and we have the access we need. It does not start at payment. We confirm your start date in writing.

For 30 Days of Store Content, delivery takes 5 business days from that start date.

Monthly continuation

After delivery, we may offer a monthly continuation plan. Its price and terms are shown to you before you pay for it.

Contact

For questions, refund requests or revision requests, use the BITS of Tampa Bay contact page.
